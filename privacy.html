Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026  |  Company: Alisa Finds

Alisa Finds ("we," "our," or "us") operates this website and the internal Alisa Finds Content Manager application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring complete transparency about how our website and internal publishing workflows operate.

1. Pinterest API Usage & First-Party Data Policy

The Alisa Finds Content Manager connects to the official Pinterest API v5 exclusively to manage and publish curated lifestyle content to our own authorized Pinterest Business account (@alisa_finds).

  • No Third-Party User Data Collection: We do not use the Pinterest API to collect, store, process, sell, or share any personal data belonging to Pinterest users or visitors.
  • Internal Authentication Only: OAuth 2.0 authentication is restricted strictly to the first-party administrator of Alisa Finds. All API credentials and tokens are stored in encrypted environment variables and are never exposed publicly.
  • No External API Sharing: We do not grant third parties access to our Pinterest API integration.

2. Website Information We Collect

Our website (Alisa Finds) is a static curation showcase hosted via GitHub Pages. We do not require visitors to register accounts, submit forms, or provide personal details (such as names, emails, or payment information) to browse our website.

3. Affiliate Links & Third-Party Marketplaces

Alisa Finds participates in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites and creators to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.in and affiliated marketplaces.

  • When you click an outbound product link on our Pinterest Pins or website, you are directed to Amazon.
  • As an Amazon Associate, we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you.
  • Once you leave our website or Pinterest profile and visit Amazon, your browsing and purchase activity is governed by Amazon's own Privacy Notice and Cookie Policy.

4. Cookies & Visitor Rights

We do not deploy invasive tracking scripts or sell personal browsing data on our website. Standard hosting logs managed by GitHub Pages or platform analytics provided natively by Pinterest are governed by their respective platform privacy policies. Users may manage or opt out of personalized platform settings directly through their Pinterest account preferences or browser cookie controls.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, our data handling practices, or our Pinterest content, please reach out to us directly via our official Pinterest Business profile at pinterest.com/alisa_finds.
